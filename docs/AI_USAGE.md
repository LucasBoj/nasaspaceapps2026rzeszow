# Use of AI — session log

This is the English disclosure of every AI tool used in SELENE OPS, for the Space Apps rule on the use of AI
("Super Intelligence"). The idea, the problem definition and the product decisions are the team's. AI tools helped
build the software and made the 3D lander models. **No scientific number is AI-generated:** sunlight, Earth
visibility and battery values are computed deterministically from NASA LOLA terrain and JPL ephemerides, and lander
specifications come from cited sources. See the README section *Use of AI* for where the marks are in the app and
the files.

## 3D lander models (AI-generated content)

| Date | Tool | What |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-14 | Meshy AI | Concept images of the five landers (Nova-C, Blue Ghost, Griffin, Starship HLS, Blue Moon Mk2), generated by the team |
| 2026-11-14 | Microsoft TRELLIS.2 (MIT) | Image-to-3D models from those images, generated by the team |
| 2026-11-14 | glTF Transform 4.5.1 + meshoptimizer 1.2 (MIT) | Mesh and texture optimisation (`tools/optimize-models`); not generative AI |
| 2026-11-15 | Claude Code | Review of textures for logos and flags; the painting-out step (`tools/optimize-models/redactions.json`) |

Marked by: the in-app "AI-generated 3D model" badge, the "Contains AI-generated 3D models" watermark on screenshots
and the demo video, `asset.generator` / `asset.extras` in each GLB, and `aiGenerated` in `landers.json`.
